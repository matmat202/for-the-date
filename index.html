      Each past year contributes the 15 days centred on the date, so every comparison is against 450 days. The past comes from Open-Meteo's historical weather (ERA5 reanalysis: a model's reconstruction of past weather on a grid of about 10–25 km, not one thermometer in town), and the week from its forecast. The two are different models, so the page measures how far apart they were here over the last 60 days and takes that gap off the forecast first. Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0).
