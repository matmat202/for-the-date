    Data: CLIWOC 2.1, the Climatological Database for the World's Oceans (García-Herrera et al., Climatic Change 2005), from historicalclimatology.com. Wind force is CLIWOC's conversion of each log's own words (“fresh gales”, “bramzeilskoelte”) to metres per second, shown here on the Beaufort scale; the words are the log's. Dates are Gregorian, so a British log kept on the Julian calendar before 1752 appears under the date the rest of Europe was using. Positions are as logged, and some are on land because 18th-century longitude was a guess. Dutch, Spanish and French words are machine-translated (by Claude) and shown with the original beneath; a translation of 250-year-old sailors' shorthand can be wrong, so the original is the record. Map: Natural Earth.
