Epilogue · Reading For the Date

Usual means 1991–2020

When For the Date says warmer than usual, it means warmer than the same days were in 1991–2020, the normal climatologists use. Lately that is the call on about 4 days in 10, not 3, and in nearly every city.

Data: the 30 cities from Open-Meteo’s historical weather, model ERA5-Seamless (ERA5-Land temperatures, on a grid of about 11 km), the 30 most populous towns in For the Date’s own list, taking only the first in each country; the stations from NOAA NCEI GHCN-Daily, a date counted only where at least 300 of its 450 past days were recorded.
